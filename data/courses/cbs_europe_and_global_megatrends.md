# Europe and Global Megatrends (BA-BEBUO1019U)

## Prerequisites
Good knowledge of the European Union and of theories such as varieties of capitalism, growth models, European integration and welfare regimes.

## Topics
- Four megatrends: social change, climate crisis, technological disruption, shifts in global economic power
- Theories of the state's role in the economy
- Labour markets, natural resources, financial and commodity markets, social policy, green investment, digitalisation and cyber security, trade disputes
- Analysis at global and European level, for public and private sectors

## Learning outcomes
- Explain megatrends and their impact on society
- Analyse related policy problems with qualitative and quantitative sources
- Assess opportunities and constraints for European businesses and states
- Evaluate EU policies and how EU institutions could respond

## Teaching and assessment
Lectures, exercise classes, policy drafting. Oral group exam based on a policy-brief synopsis.
