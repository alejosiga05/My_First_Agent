# Corporate Strategy in a Network Perspective (BA-BEBUO1018U)

## Prerequisites
Basic knowledge of R (e.g. from a statistics course).

## Topics
- Theories of corporate strategy seen from a network perspective, with cases
- Network analysis methods and tools (using R)
- Analysing a real network inside or between organisations
- Strategic implications of network positions

## Learning outcomes
- Explain corporate strategy from a network perspective
- Analyse intra- and inter-organisational networks with network analysis
- Assess how positions in social, economic and political networks affect strategy
- Give examples of successful and failed strategies from a network view

## Teaching and assessment
Lectures, exercise sessions with R and network data. Group written home assignment (max 10 pages).
