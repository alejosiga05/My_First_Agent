# Marketing (30015)

## Prerequisites
None listed.

## Topics
- Marketing strategy and marketing plans
- Market environment and information gathering
- Customer insights and consumer buying behaviour
- Business (B2B) markets and buying behaviour
- Segmentation, targeting and positioning
- Product strategy and branding
- Pricing
- Distribution channels, wholesale and retail
- Integrated marketing communications

## Learning outcomes
- Explain how marketing creates value for customers, firms and society
- Design basic marketing research
- Segment markets, choose targets and define positioning
- Make marketing-mix decisions (product, price, promotion, place) and build a branding strategy

## Teaching and assessment
Lectures with business cases, in-class exercises, group marketing plan. Attending: 50% group marketing plan, 50% final exam.
