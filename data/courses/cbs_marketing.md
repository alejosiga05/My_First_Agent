# Marketing (BA-BIBAO4021U)

## Prerequisites
Not stated.

## Topics
- Marketing management fundamentals, with a focus on Asian markets
- Market research tools; analysis of consumer and business (B2B) markets
- Creating, communicating and delivering customer value
- Segmentation, targeting, positioning, brand equity and value propositions
- Competitors, institutions, consumer culture and internal resources as constraints on market choices
- Adapting marketing plans and communication to target markets
- Two group cases (one B2C, one B2B) with empirical study and strategy

## Learning outcomes
- Understand marketing management theories, models and analysis methods
- Carry out market research and analyse consumer and business markets
- Design an empirical study and turn the results into marketing strategies and plans
- Discuss current issues such as digitalisation, sustainability and ethics

## Teaching and assessment
Lectures, exercise classes, two group case assignments. Individual written home assignment (max 10 pages).
