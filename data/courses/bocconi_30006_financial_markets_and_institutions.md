# Financial Markets and Institutions (30006)

## Prerequisites
Applied mathematics, management, accounting and financial statement analysis.

## Topics
- Role of financial markets and institutions in the economy
- Main financial instruments (bonds, stocks, foreign exchange, derivatives) and how their markets are organised
- Regulated vs over-the-counter markets
- Goals and organisation of commercial banks, investment banks and mutual funds
- Regulation of banks and other financial intermediaries

## Learning outcomes
- Explain how financial markets work and what financial institutions do in the economy
- Interpret and evaluate the behaviour of financial institutions

## Teaching and assessment
Recorded lectures, individual and group assignments, a class presentation, online problem sets. Final exam plus participation and presentation.
