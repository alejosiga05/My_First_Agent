# Organization Theory (30153)

## Prerequisites
None.

## Topics
- Individual behaviour: personality, perception, emotions, decision-making, learning, motivation
- Groups and teams: structure, dynamics, communication
- Leadership, power and influence
- Organisational structure, culture and change
- Human resource management

## Learning outcomes
- Apply models of individual, group and organisational behaviour
- Analyse team dynamics and choose a leadership style
- Design an organisational structure and HRM systems

## Teaching and assessment
Lectures, exercises, simulations and role-play, cases. Single written exam (multiple choice and open questions).
