# Corporate Valuation (30257)

## Prerequisites
Basic financial accounting and basic corporate finance.

## Topics
- Purposes of valuation and overview of valuation approaches
- Financial analysis and cash flows for valuation
- Business plans and financial modelling
- Discounted cash flow (DCF) models; value, leverage and growth
- Weighted average cost of capital (WACC)
- Valuation with multiples
- Scenario analysis and Monte Carlo simulation
- Valuation in M&A and rights issues
- Climate-related issues in finance

## Learning outcomes
- Build and interpret DCF and multiples valuations and compare their results
- Choose the valuation method that fits the purpose and the right data inputs
- Write a valuation memo to international standards

## Teaching and assessment
Lectures, Excel modelling sessions, guest speakers, group valuation project. Written exam plus group assignment.
