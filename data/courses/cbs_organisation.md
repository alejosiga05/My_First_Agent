# Organisation (BA-BIBAO3023U)

## Prerequisites
Not stated.

## Topics
- Organisation theory and organisational behaviour for international managers in Asia
- Methods to analyse organisational structure and behaviour
- Organising markets, stakeholders, employees, alliances and subsidiaries
- Group case on a multinational company

## Learning outcomes
- Formulate and answer a research question on organisational design and behaviour
- Build a framework of key organisation theories and models
- Apply it to problems of multinationals and critically assess its assumptions

## Teaching and assessment
Lectures, workshops, cases, guest speakers. Oral exam based on a group synopsis.
