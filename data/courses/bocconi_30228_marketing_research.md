# Marketing Research (30228)

## Prerequisites
Basic statistics and prior empirical courses; Excel and SPSS used in class.

## Topics
- Exploratory, descriptive and qualitative research
- Survey research and questionnaire design
- Causal research
- Customer segmentation
- Conjoint analysis
- Experimental design, field experiments and A/B testing
- Working with real-world data

## Learning outcomes
- Choose the right research method for a marketing problem
- Judge the quality and limits of existing marketing research
- Design a research question, collect and analyse data, and report results

## Teaching and assessment
Lectures, exercises with Excel and SPSS, guest speakers, research project. Attending: assignments 40%, final exam 60%.
