# Financial Statements Analysis (30024)

## Prerequisites
Solid basic financial accounting (preparing and reading financial statements). Corporate finance and valuation helpful but not required.

## Topics
- Review of core accounting concepts
- Financial statement analysis: profitability, liquidity, solvency
- Earnings quality: drivers, consequences and how to measure it
- Detecting fraud and bankruptcy risk
- Forecasting company performance
- Firm valuation techniques, with attention to sustainable investing
- Going concern and material uncertainty
- Role of analysts, investor relations and other actors in financial information

## Learning outcomes
- Understand accounting standards, disclosure and how financial information is produced
- Analyse real company cases and assess earnings quality
- Carry out a firm valuation and make an investment recommendation

## Teaching and assessment
Lectures, cases, guest speakers, role-play on valuation and deal negotiation. Attending: in-class test 25%, role-play 25%, final exam 50%.
