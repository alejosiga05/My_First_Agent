# Principles of Capital Markets (Finance: Markets, Institutions and Instruments, Course I) (0996)

## Prerequisites
Admission to the Finance: Markets, Institutions and Instruments specialization; basic Excel or R and statistics recommended.

## Topics
- Introduction to finance: returns, interest rates, discounting, net present value
- Utility, risk preferences and diversification
- Mean-variance trade-off, efficient frontier, tangency portfolio
- CAPM, estimating beta and empirical tests
- Factor models; systematic vs unsystematic risk

## Learning outcomes
- Explain the risk-return relationship of financial assets
- Compute risk and return of single assets and portfolios
- Find optimal portfolio weights and use diversification in investment decisions
- Discuss the assumptions and limits of the CAPM and decompose risk into market and firm-specific parts

## Teaching and assessment
Large lecture plus small interactive groups with student mini-cases. Final exam 40%, interactive part 60%.
