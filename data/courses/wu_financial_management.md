# Financial Management (1775)

## Prerequisites
None listed.

## Topics
- Core concepts: cost of capital, valuation, internal rate of return, bond yields, uncertainty
- Expected return and risk of portfolios (scenario-based and historical data)
- Market vs firm-specific risk, diversification, beta, efficient portfolios
- Portfolio choice with and without a risk-free asset; Sharpe ratio
- Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) in practice
- Capital structure and cost of capital: cost of equity, cost of debt, WACC
- Applications: dividend discount model, capital budgeting, business valuation with DCF

## Learning outcomes
- Use expected value, variance, covariance and correlation in finance problems
- Value bonds and assess credit risk with ratings
- Measure and optimise portfolio risk and return; estimate beta and apply the CAPM
- Compute cost of equity, cost of debt and WACC (including the tax shield of debt)
- Value shares, investment projects and companies with risk-adjusted discount rates
- Solve practical problems in Excel with financial data

## Teaching and assessment
Self-study, Q&A sessions, numerical examples, two team projects. Final exam 60%, team projects 20% + 20%.
