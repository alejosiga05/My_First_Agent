# Strategic Management and Organizational Design (Specialization in Business Administration, Course IV) (0727)

## Prerequisites
Not stated.

## Topics
- Analysis of the external environment: industry, competitors, macro-environment
- Analysis of internal capabilities, resources, structure and culture
- Developing and evaluating strategic responses to business challenges
- Aligning strategy, organisational structure, culture and change
- Business cases and group projects

## Learning outcomes
- Understand core concepts of strategic management and organisational design
- Analyse external and internal factors and their strategic implications
- Identify strategic challenges and develop feasible responses
- Apply strategy frameworks to real cases; teamwork and presentation skills

## Teaching and assessment
Lectures, case studies, group exercises, peer feedback. Group assignments and final group project, plus individual report and peer evaluation.
