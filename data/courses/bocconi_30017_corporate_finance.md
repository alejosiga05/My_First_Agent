# Corporate Finance (30017)

## Prerequisites
Main financial ratios (profitability, liquidity, turnover, financial structure); time value of money; basic descriptive statistics.

## Topics
- Goals of the firm, corporate governance and introduction to corporate finance
- Financial statement analysis: reclassification, cash flow statement, long-term financial planning
- Valuing bonds and shares
- Capital budgeting and investment decision rules
- Risk analysis and real options in investment decisions
- Risk and return, the CAPM
- Cost of capital and estimation of beta
- Capital structure and firm value (basic models), debt capacity and financial distress
- How cost of capital, firm value and investment decisions are linked

## Learning outcomes
- Distinguish accounting profit from cash flow; understand time value of money and the risk-return trade-off
- Compute cash flows, discount them and evaluate investment projects
- Estimate cost of equity and WACC from market data
- Value bonds, shares and firms, and assess the effect of leverage on firm value

## Teaching and assessment
Lectures, exercises, guest speakers, group assignment. Written exam (multiple choice, open questions, one exercise); attending students also get a group assignment grade.
