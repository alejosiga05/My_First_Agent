# Principles of Corporate Finance (BA-BIBAO3021U)

## Prerequisites
Basic managerial accounting/accounting/controlling and Excel (can be learned in parallel).

## Topics
- What corporate finance is and the role of the financial manager
- Measuring company performance: value added, efficiency, profitability
- Time value of money
- Valuing bonds and shares with present value methods
- Capital budgeting and comparing investment rules, including under capital rationing
- Cost of capital, capital structure, risk and return
- Introduction to options

## Learning outcomes
- Define and discuss the main corporate finance concepts and models
- Choose and apply the right financial technique to a problem
- Analyse decisions from a CFO's point of view and interpret the results

## Teaching and assessment
Blended lectures, exercises and cases. 4-hour individual written exam.
