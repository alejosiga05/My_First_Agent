# IFRS Accounting (Specialization in Business Administration, Course I: International Accounting and Controlling) (0623)

## Prerequisites
Good knowledge of double-entry bookkeeping and single-entity financial statements; an undergraduate financial accounting course.

## Topics
- Overview of IFRS financial reporting: objectives and basic principles
- The IASB and how standards are set
- The Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting
- Selected standards in detail: IFRS 18, IAS 16 (property, plant and equipment), IAS 38 (intangible assets)
- Recognition and measurement, including fair value

## Learning outcomes
- Understand the regulatory framework and main elements of IFRS reporting
- Use the Conceptual Framework to analyse reporting questions
- Apply IFRS recognition and measurement rules and recognise where judgement is needed
- Critically read IFRS financial statements, including accounting discretion and managers' reporting incentives

## Teaching and assessment
On-campus lectures with practical examples. Quizzes 10%, two tests 45% each.
