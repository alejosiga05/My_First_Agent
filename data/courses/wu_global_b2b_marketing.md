# Global B-2-B Marketing (0015)

## Prerequisites
None.

## Topics
- Specific features of business-to-business (B2B) marketing in a global setting
- B2B marketing strategy, branding and communication
- Neuromarketing and emotions in B2B
- Digital B2B marketing and social media for business customers
- Case studies and best practices

## Learning outcomes
- Identify the specific challenges of B2B marketing
- Compare B2B and B2C marketing and transfer B2C know-how to B2B
- Analyse B2B best-practice cases

## Teaching and assessment
Lectures, discussions, case presentations. Participation 20%, group presentations 40%, final paper 40%.
