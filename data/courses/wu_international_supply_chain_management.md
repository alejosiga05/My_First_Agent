# International Supply Chain Management (1708)

## Prerequisites
Attendance at the first class is mandatory.

## Topics
- What a supply chain is and how it works
- Bullwhip effect (Beer Game) and supply chain coordination
- Supply chain strategy and network design
- Global supply chains, globalisation and international trade; supply chain drivers and metrics
- Decision trees; planning demand and supply

## Learning outcomes
- Understand supply chain concepts and their link to strategy and operations
- Identify competitive advantages from good supply chain management
- Analyse how globalisation and trade affect international logistics
- Plan and design an efficient supply chain network

## Teaching and assessment
Hybrid classes with case studies and company speakers. Assignments and cases 40%, take-home exam 60%.
