# Financial Statement Analysis (KAN-CFIAO1004U)

## Prerequisites
Not stated.

## Topics
- Refresher on the income statement, balance sheet, equity statement and cash flow statement; accrual vs cash accounting
- Profitability analysis, including ROIC and EVA
- Growth analysis and value-creating growth
- Forecasting and pro forma financial statements
- Firm valuation approaches and their pros and cons
- Accounting quality, accounting flexibility and how to adjust for its misuse

## Learning outcomes
- Read and understand financial statements
- Analyse profitability and growth with ratios
- Build pro forma statements and estimate firm value
- Assess accounting quality and adjust for manipulation

## Teaching and assessment
Blended lectures, exercise classes and workshops. 4-hour individual written exam.
