# Business Strategy (30012)

## Prerequisites
No formal prerequisites; prior economics, marketing, finance and operations courses strongly recommended.

## Topics
- Competitive advantage and what drives it
- Industry and competitive environment analysis
- Generic strategies
- Resources and capabilities; activity systems
- Industry dynamics and changing competition
- Entry, attack and defence strategies; strategic renewal
- Sustainability of competitive advantage
- Cooperative strategy and alliances (exchange-student classes)

## Learning outcomes
- Explain industry attractiveness and sources of competitive advantage
- Analyse a firm's strategy against competitive forces and industry dynamics
- Evaluate sustainability of advantage and growth options
- Apply concepts to real cases in written and oral analyses

## Teaching and assessment
Lectures, team case discussions, group assignments, guest managers, strategy simulation. Exchange classes: 20% participation and quizzes, 80% group case analyses.
