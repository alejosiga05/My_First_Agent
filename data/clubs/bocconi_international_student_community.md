# International and exchange student community at Bocconi

## ESN Bocconi Milano (Erasmus Student Network)
- The local section of the Erasmus Student Network at Bocconi, for exchange and international students.
- Activities: sport (football tournaments, sport days), parties, trips, cultural events, and information and support during the whole semester.
- Website: https://milanobocconi.esn.it (Bocconi's page lists www.esnbocconi.it)
- Instagram: @esnbocconi
- Facebook: ESN Bocconi Milano - Erasmus Student Network
- App: ESNapp (follow ESN Bocconi)
- ESN Bocconi is one of six ESN sections in Milan, together called "ESN a Milano Unita" (also ESN Milano-Bicocca, Politecnico, Statale, Unicatt and IULM).
- Bocconi page about ESN Bocconi: https://www.unibocconi.it/it/studenti-iscritti/campus-life/esn-bocconi-erasmus-student-network

## Bocconi University official social media
- Instagram: @unibocconi (official university account)

## How to find WhatsApp or other group chats safely
- Ask ESN Bocconi (website, Instagram or at their events) for the official groups for your semester.
- Ask the association directly, at Associations on Display (September and February) or through its official social media.
- Be careful with groups found on random websites or shared by strangers, especially if someone offers housing and asks for money.
