# How to join a Bocconi student association

- Student associations present themselves at "Associations on Display", a fair held every year in September and in February. In 2026 the February edition ran from 9 to 13 February, 10:00 am to 6:30 pm, in the Leonardo Del Vecchio Building (source: https://www.unibocconi.it/en/events/associations-display-0).
- Associations usually open their recruitment (application campaigns) during Associations on Display, so that is the best moment to join. Exchange students arriving in September or February can go to the fair in their first weeks.
- The official annual list shows all active associations and their topic areas.
- To learn more about one association, contact it directly through its social media or website.
- The official page does not describe special rules for exchange students.
- Any Bocconi student can also create a new association (at least 10 founders, a statute, 150 supporting signatures, application in June); this is unlikely to fit a one-semester exchange.
