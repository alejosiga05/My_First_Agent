# Housing information for incoming exchange students

- Incoming exchange students can reserve a room in a student residence hall on campus or in its immediate surroundings.
- For academic year 2026-27 exchange students have their own dedicated housing application, separate from other applications, managed directly by the Bocconi Housing Office.
- The Housing Office contacts nominated exchange students before the application opens and sends instructions.
- Housing is not guaranteed. Rooms are assigned first come, first served, so applying early matters.
- This page does not cover prices, room types, private housing or search tips. For those, see the Bocconi exchange housing page for the semester, or contact the Housing Office through https://www.unibocconi.it/en/contact-us
