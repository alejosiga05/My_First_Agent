# Housing for incoming exchange students – Fall 2026-27

## Who can apply
- Incoming exchange students at Bocconi for the Fall semester 2026-27.
- You must first complete the "ISD – Exchange Program – Fall 2026" application. Only after that can you open the housing application.

## Residences and room types reserved for exchange students
- Aparto Giovenale: single room with private bathroom (ensuite) or shared twin room (2 beds, bathroom inside the room). About a 5-minute walk from campus. Owned and managed by an external company, in partnership with Bocconi.
- Spadolini: single room in a 4-bedroom apartment, bathroom shared with one other student, shared kitchen and living room in the apartment.
- Dubini: same layout as Spadolini.
- Javotte: single room, bathroom shared with one other student, shared kitchen and living room on the floor.
- Apartments can be shared with regularly enrolled (degree-seeking) Bocconi students.
- Bocconi residence halls have study rooms, common areas, living rooms and games rooms.

## Period
- Semester only: 24 August 2026 to 24 December 2026.

## Prices for the whole period (bills included)
- Aparto Giovenale ensuite: 4,770 EUR
- Aparto Giovenale twin: 3,025 EUR per bed (extra cleaning available for a fee)
- Spadolini and Dubini: 4,250 EUR (maintenance and weekly cleaning included)
- Javotte: 3,850 EUR (maintenance and weekly cleaning included)

## How to apply (Fall 2026-27)
1. Complete the ISD exchange program application first.
2. The housing application opened on Tuesday 26 May 2026 at 3:00 pm Italian time and closed on Wednesday 27 May 2026 at 11:59 pm Italian time.
3. You enter a virtual waiting room; when it is your turn you log in with your Bocconi user ID and password.
4. Choose a residence and room type, click "Save", then "Submit data". If the "Submit data" button disappears and new sections appear, the room is reserved.
5. Complete the remaining sections before the final deadline.
- If "Save" or "Submit data" fails, that room type is full: choose another one.

## Important rules
- First come, first served. Rooms are limited and opening the application does not guarantee a room.
- Reservations made through any other channel are cancelled. An Aparto room is only valid if you first complete the Bocconi housing application and then the Aparto portal; the Housing Office sends Aparto the official list and any reservation not on it is cancelled automatically.
- The page does not give the Spring 2026-27 dates.
