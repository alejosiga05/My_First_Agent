## Safety tips (general advice from the project team, not from Bocconi)
- Only trust Bocconi information on the official domain unibocconi.it. Look-alike domains (for example ones ending in ".it.com") are not Bocconi.
- Do not pay a deposit or rent before you have seen a written contract and verified the landlord or the platform.
- Be careful with offers much cheaper than the market, or landlords who cannot show the flat (in person or by live video).
