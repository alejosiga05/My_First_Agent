# Bocconi residence halls – overview

- Bocconi has 7 university residence halls. Together with other Bocconi housing they host up to about 1,700 students.
- All residences are in the city centre, at most a 10–15 minute walk from the buildings where lectures take place.
- All residences offer single bedrooms, with a private bathroom or a bathroom shared with at most one other student, and access to a shared kitchen.
- Residence life is international: students from many countries live together.
- For degree-seeking students in 2026-27 the room assignment runs from 20 August 2026 to 27 June 2027. Places are limited.
- In 2026 Bocconi reserved about 700 additional beds for its students at the Olympic Village in Milan (source: https://www.unibocconi.it/en/current-students/housing).
- Exchange students do not use this application: they apply through the dedicated exchange incoming housing application.
